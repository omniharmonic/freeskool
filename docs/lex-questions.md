# Free School — four questions for Lex

*Benjamin Life (@omniharmonic) · 2026-09-12 · draft for Benjamin's approval before sending · plain language, no protocol jargon*

## 1. Feedback: counts and quiet notes, not scores

You and I both said no to Uber-style ratings. The plan is: after a class, people who came can leave **anonymous** feedback with three quick dials (knew the material / taught it well / the experience) and an optional note. The host only ever sees a **summary once at least a few people have answered** (we're using five for anything with words, three for the dials), never who said what, never a running tally, and never a public number. Organizers can see more if there's a safety problem. Publicly, a host's page shows **counts**: classes hosted, classes attended, how many people vouched for them on a skill. Does that compromise sit right with you, or does even the private summary feel too much like grading?

## 2. Who can post a class on day one

Two ways to set this up; you can change it later from the admin page. **Open** (your "if you say you're Free School, you're Free School"): anyone with a profile can post a class immediately. **Gated**: you need an invite link or a vouch to make a profile, and you need to have come to two classes before you can host. Our default is open; the gate is a switch you flip if spam or bad actors show up. Which do you want for the launch at COhere?

## 3. What's public on a profile by default

The privacy review was blunt: this runs on an open network where anything public is copied and kept forever, and your network includes people with legal exposure. So the defaults we're proposing are: the **calendar is public** (class name, time, neighborhood, not exact address until you've RSVP'd); **who RSVPs and who attended is not public**; **your profile shows a made-up name and no photo unless you add them**; **skills you want to teach are visible to the school, and you tick a box to make them public** (ordinary skills are pre-ticked, anything legally sensitive like street medic or ICE response is not). New people get a brand-new Free School account by default; signing in with an existing Bluesky account is possible but comes with a plain warning that it links the two forever. Anything there you'd set differently?

## 4. freeschool.com and names

You said you own freeschool.com. Can you confirm, and would you want the app there (boulder.freeschool.com, asheville.freeschool.com, Craigslist-style)? Two cautions from the research: people's **usernames should not live on that domain** (once you give out name@freeschool.com handles they're gone forever, and the domain becomes a roster anyone can list), and the **server people's accounts live on should have a boring, unrelated name**, because the network publishes which server hosts each account. So: freeschool.com for the website and the school's own account, something neutral for member accounts. Good with you?

One more, small: the supplies-donation field. Options are (a) none, (b) a "bring $5 for vegetables" note the host types, (c) a Venmo link. We lean (b).
