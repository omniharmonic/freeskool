# Free School — publication-tier and interoperability audit

*Benjamin Life (@omniharmonic) · 2026-09-13 · refinement phase, task 19. Companion: `docs/architecture.md`, `docs/prd.md`, `docs/research-brief-v0.2.md`, `docs/lucian-review-packet.md`. This document audited only; every gap is listed in §4, and **§6 records the six fixed on 2026-09-13** — each gap in §4 now carries a status line.*

## 1. Summary

Free School composes correctly on the commons — every class is a plain `community.lexicon.calendar.event` in its **host's own repo**, with `coop.lexicon.event.config`, `freeschool.draft.skillLevel` and `freeschool.draft.series` attached as strongRef sidecars, and the school's only additions are its own `coop.lexicon.event.listing` curation records and materialized occurrences, written through `SchoolActorPort` (`apps/appview/src/lib/events.ts:1-23`, `:201`). The placement rules from R9 hold in code and are enforced by a scripted check (`apps/appview/scripts/privacy-audit.ts`): RSVPs, attendance, feedback, roster, moderation reasons and attestations are app-side, and the one derived role that reaches the protocol is triple-gated (`apps/appview/src/lib/membership-claims.ts:141-199`). The honest finding is that **the top tier is empty**: `PDS_CRAWLERS=` in `infra/production/.env.example:21` means our production PDS announces to no relay, so nothing we publish is on the firehose at all — every "public" row in the matrix below is public-by-permission but reachable today only by someone who already knows to fetch `pds.freeskool.xyz`. The second finding is that peer federation is **one-directional and invisible on the protocol**: we consume peers over `subscribeRepos` (`apps/appview/src/sync/`), but a peer cannot discover our school DID, our routing tags, or our peer list through any public endpoint, and `freeschool.draft.school#peers`/`#tags` are written once by `create-school` and never edited again (`apps/appview/src/http/routes/admin.ts:354` updates only the local `fs_peer` table). The third was narrower and more urgent for the COhere test: **materialized occurrences of a recurring class were listed without tags** (`apps/appview/src/jobs/materialize-series.ts`), so a tag-routing peer would have dropped every instance of every recurring class we run — fixed in §6, along with the cid-less moderation strongRef that would have made a steward's removal un-propagatable.

## 2. The tier matrix

Four tiers. **Firehose** = any ATProto app via a relay or Jetstream. **Peer skools** = another Free School AppView that has our PDS host in its peer registry (it sees *every* public record in the repos it follows — there is no per-collection peer filter). **Within skool** = served by `/api/*` to a signed-in member. **Not published** = never leaves our Postgres.

Because `PDS_CRAWLERS` is empty, the Firehose column reads *"eligible, not announced"* for every public record: the permission is there, the relay is not. That is one switch, not fifty.

## 3. Interoperability inventory

### (a) Outbound — what another ATProto app sees

A generic calendar client that reads a **member's repo** sees: `community.lexicon.calendar.event` records with `name`, `description`, `createdAt`, `startsAt`, `endsAt`, `mode`, `status`, `locations[]`, `uris[]`, `rsvpExpected` (`lib/events.ts:265-277`) — all canonical fields, confirmed against `docs/research/briefs/r2_lexicon-inventory.md:76`; no field of ours is ever added to it. Alongside them: `coop.lexicon.event.config`, `freeschool.draft.skillLevel`, `freeschool.draft.series`, `freeschool.draft.skillClaim`, `freeschool.draft.request`, `freeschool.draft.claim`, `freeschool.draft.resource`, optionally `community.lexicon.calendar.rsvp` and `freeschool.draft.approval`. Reading the **school's repo** gives `freeschool.draft.school` (`rkey=self`), the current `freeschool.draft.policy`, every `coop.lexicon.event.listing`, materialized occurrence events and their `freeschool.draft.occurrence` back-pointers, opt-in `coop.lexicon.membership` claims, and the enum-only `freeschool.draft.moderationAction` records.

Two caveats on validity. First, **nothing validates**: `lib/pds.ts:156` and every direct `putRecord` call pass `validate: false`, because our `freeschool.draft.*` lexicons are not resolvable on the network — but that also means our *borrowed* `community.lexicon.calendar.event` writes are never checked against the canonical schema either. Second, the `coop.lexicon.*` shapes are assumptions, documented at one chokepoint (`apps/appview/src/lexicons/coop.ts:1-8`); they were read from `technefoundation/regenOS@c3e34d4`, not from a resolvable lexicon.

**Tag routing** is the COhere path (PRD §4.1 item 11, F12). `routesOnTags` (`lib/events.ts:167`) matches the event's config tags case-insensitively against the school's own `freeschool.draft.school#tags`, falling back to `DEFAULT_ROUTING_TAGS = ['skillshare', 'free-school']` (`:165`). Only a `listed` event with a matching tag gets a school listing (`:201-223`); an untagged class still appears on **our** calendar because inclusion is decided by authorship, not by the listing (`http/visibility.ts:104-111`). `decideListingEdit` (`:236`) makes a steward's removal sticky. This is correct and well-tested — except that occurrences bypass it entirely (gap 2) and moderation listings are written with a `cid`-less strongRef (gap 3).

Smoke Signal is sunset, so the calendar-consumer audience is atmo/Dandelion/Beacon-style readers and COhere's AppView; the Arbiter has no license and no membership API as of 2026-09-12, so nothing outbound targets it.

### (b) Inbound — what we ingest, and what a second skool would need

`PEER_PDS_HOSTS` seeds `fs_peer` (`index/peers.ts:26`); contrail's `relays` option is, for us, the list of PDS hosts to ask `com.atproto.sync.listReposByCollection` (`contrail.config.ts:37-38`), with a `listRepos` fallback for hosts that do not serve it (`index/discovery-fallback.ts`, probed by `peers.ts:81`). Live updates come from our own `PdsChangeSource` — one `com.atproto.sync.subscribeRepos` socket per peer host, per-host cursors, `#info`/`OutdatedCursor` repair, `#identity` re-resolution (`src/sync/README.md`, `index/live-sync.ts`) — because contrail's only shipped live source is Bluesky Jetstream, which cannot see a PDS no relay crawls. The 15-minute backfill remains the safety net. `jetstreams: ['wss://jetstream1.us-east.bsky.network']` is still configured (`contrail.config.ts:43`) but `CONTRAIL_LIVE_INGEST=false` in production.

A second skool instance federating with us today would need, in order: (1) our PDS host in its `PEER_PDS_HOSTS`; (2) nothing else — it will then index **every** public record in every repo on our PDS, including our members' skill claims and requests, because peer following is per-host, not per-collection or per-school. (3) To be seen by us, a steward must add its host through `PUT /api/admin/peers`. What breaks: there is no handshake, no public advertisement of our school DID or routing tags, and `school#peers` — the record that was supposed to make federation transitive (`architecture.md:137`, brief §5 "Peer registry") — is written empty by `create-school` and never updated. Two schools on the *same* PDS (the Boulder + demo case) are indistinguishable to a peer except by reading each `freeschool.draft.school` record.

### (c) Identity and portability

Primary door: a custodial account on our PDS with a generated (soon chosen — spec §3 R-5) handle under `PDS_HANDLE_DOMAIN`; secondary door: server-side OAuth against the member's existing PDS. Both produce a real DID on the production PLC. `takeOwnership` (`lib/custody.ts:285-360`) rotates the PDS password, flips `isCustodial`, shows the new password once, and tells the member in plain words that they can export the whole repo with `com.atproto.sync.getRepo`. After that, our `actorAgent` returns `NoActorCredentialError` until they sign back in by OAuth — i.e. they are an ordinary external account.

**Can a member move to another PDS and keep their history?** Yes for everything that is a record: events, configs, sidecars, skill claims, requests, claims, resources and public RSVPs all live in their repo and migrate with it. No for everything app-side: RSVPs, attendance, vouches, badges, feedback and role are ours, keyed by DID, and a PDS migration keeps the DID — so the *derived* history survives on **this** AppView but does not travel to another one. That is the intended trade (R9), but it should be said out loud in the take-ownership copy.

### (d) Skill profiles and attestations across skools

Claims travel: `freeschool.draft.skillClaim` is in the member's repo, one record per skill (`routes/me.ts:491`), with `skill` as an at-uri into the shared taxonomy — so a second skool indexing that repo resolves the same skill node and can render the same claim without asking us anything. Withdrawal is real: the PUT reads the member's own repo and deletes every claim not in the new set (`:576-607`).

Attestations do **not** travel. They are app-side rows (`routes/attestations.ts`, spec §3 R-2), positive-only, attester-removable; counts are shown to members, attester identity only to the subject. The privacy audit fails closed on any `freeschool.draft.skillAttestation` record found in a repo (`scripts/privacy-audit.ts:34-38`). The path to cross-skool badges, unchanged from the packet (§"Extend-vs-sidecar", row `skillAttestation`), is **double opt-in**: the attester writes `skillAttestation {subject, skill, direction: positive, context?}` into *their own* repo, and the subject writes a matching acceptance into *theirs*; neither record alone is a public statement about anyone, and an AppView pairs them. Under R9 this is the only construction that satisfies "no public record may name a DID its holder did not write" — the attester's record names a DID, but it becomes *visible as an attestation* only when the subject's own record completes the pair. The consent table and the acceptance record shape both need to exist before the audit's exemption 3 can be turned on, and neither does yet.

### (e) Badges and roles

Roles are a pure function over evidence and policy (`deriveRole` in `packages/shared`); badges are counts. The only thing a peer can derive from our public records is: how many classes a DID hosted (count their events), how many requests they claimed, what skills they claim, and — when all three gates hold — an explicit `coop.lexicon.membership {subject, role, school, addedBy}` naming them at role ≥ 20. A peer **cannot** derive attendance, vouches, or feedback, and that is by design. The role integer registry (10/20/30/40) is documented only in `lexicons/coop.ts:46`; a peer reading `role: 20` has to know our ladder.

## 4. Gap list

Severity: **P1** blocks the COhere test or leaves a stated capability non-functional; **P2** degrades a real consumer; **P3** polish or documentation.

1. **P1 — the firehose tier is empty.** `PDS_CRAWLERS=` (`infra/production/.env.example:21`). No relay crawls `pds.freeskool.xyz`, so nothing we publish reaches Jetstream, and the PRD's "Smoke Signal's former users and atmo/Dandelion/Beacon readers see correct events" (§6) is false in production. *Consumer hit:* an atmo user searching the network for Boulder classes finds nothing. *Proposed change:* none in code — a documented decision. Either set `PDS_CRAWLERS=https://bsky.network` and accept that the PDS hostname becomes enumerable through relay-crawled repos, or keep it empty and state in `docs/architecture.md` §1 that the top trust-boundary row means "public by permission, distributed by explicit peering only". Add a line to `infra/production/.env.example` recording which was chosen and why. *Decides:* **Benjamin** (this is the R9 neutral-hostname trade, and `PDS_HANDLE_DOMAIN=freeskool.xyz` already leaks the school in every handle).
   *Status: open — decision: Benjamin.*
2. **P1 — occurrence listings carry no tags.** `jobs/materialize-series.ts:263-269` writes `coop.lexicon.event.listing` with no `tags` field, unconditionally, for every materialized occurrence — bypassing `routeListing`'s tag gate entirely. *Consumer hit:* COhere filters incoming listings on `skillshare`/`free-school`; every instance of every recurring class is dropped, while the one-off classes come through. Conversely, occurrences of an *untagged* series are listed when they should not be. *Proposed change:* thread the series template's config tags into `materializeSeries` and call `routeListing()` instead of the inline `putRecordAsSchool`, so occurrences obey exactly the same gate as their template. Files: `apps/appview/src/jobs/materialize-series.ts`, `apps/appview/src/lib/events.ts` (export the shape already there). ## 5. Open questions for Benjamin

1. **Relay or not?** Setting `PDS_CRAWLERS=https://bsky.network` is the difference between "an ATProto-native app" and "an app that happens to use ATProto records". It makes every public record globally distributed and permanently re-broadcastable, and it makes the PDS hostname discoverable — the exact thing the R9 neutral-hostname recommendation was protecting. It is also the only way F12's "COhere's AppView indexes it" works without COhere adding us as an explicit peer. Which do you want for launch?
2. **Is `pds.freeskool.xyz` the neutral hostname R9 asked for?** It is not neutral — it says "free skool" in the DID document of every member. If the answer is "we accepted that", say so in `infra/production/.env.example` and close the question; if not, it must change before real members exist, because DIDs are forever.
3. **Should the public calendar API return class descriptions?** (Gap 5.) Withholding them protects nothing a peer cannot read anyway, and it makes anyone syndicating our calendar look broken.
4. **Should the school publish its peer list?** (Gap 4b.) A public `school#peers` array is how federation becomes transitive and how another city can find us — and it is also a public statement that Boulder Free School is affiliated with each of those schools.
5. **Does the R9 rule mean "did not write" or "did not consent to"?** (Gap 11.) The opt-in role claim is live in code and exempted in the audit; the wording in CLAUDE.md forbids it as written.
6. **COhere:** which tags, and when is the joint listing test with Aaron Gabriel? Gaps 2 and 3 must land before it, and gap 7 (the unvalidated `coop.lexicon.*` shapes) is the one that could invalidate everything we have written so far.
7. **Is a public `.ics` feed in scope for v1?** (Gap 12.) It is a day of work and reaches every organizer in Boulder who will never install a PWA.
